Create one independent high-end editorial artwork for each uploaded photograph.

Each uploaded photograph must be processed individually and output as a separate, fully finished illustration.

**IMPORTANT OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS**

- Generate ONLY the final transformed illustration.

- Do NOT display the original photograph.

- Do NOT create before-and-after comparisons.

- Do NOT divide the canvas into multiple sections.

- Do NOT use split-screen layouts, collages, diptychs, or comparison panels.

- Do NOT place the original photograph inside the illustration.

- The entire canvas must contain one unified, finished editorial illustration.

- Use the uploaded photograph strictly as a visual reference for the transformation.

## OVERALL FORMAT

Strict 3:4 vertical composition.

Create a single, cohesive minimalist hand-drawn editorial illustration that fills the entire canvas.

The artwork should resemble a refined independent art book cover, contemporary illustration, or thoughtfully designed paper publication.

Maintain a strong sense of artistic balance, generous negative space, and quiet visual storytelling.

## MAIN ILLUSTRATION — MINIMAL HAND-DRAWN PAPER ART

Extract the most recognizable visual elements from the uploaded photograph and reinterpret them as a minimalist hand-drawn paper-cover illustration.

Preserve:

- The most recognizable subject

- Essential silhouette and proportions

- Key pose or gesture

- Important objects and accessories

- Distinctive clothing and recognizable visual characteristics

- The core narrative relationship between people and objects

- The overall atmosphere and emotional character of the original scene

Highly simplify the image.

Remove unnecessary photographic details while retaining the visual information needed for immediate recognition.

The illustration must remain clearly connected to the original photograph.

Do not invent unrelated objects, change the main subject, or significantly alter its identity or pose.

## ILLUSTRATION STYLE

Use:

- Delicate, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines

- A small number of bold, clearly defined acrylic-style flat color shapes

- Rough natural paper texture

- Visible handmade brush marks

- Slightly irregular, organic edges

- Subtle imperfections that make the artwork feel genuinely handmade

- Minimal shading

- Simplified shapes and expressive silhouettes

- Restrained, sophisticated artistic detailing

Avoid photorealism, glossy digital rendering, excessive gradients, 3D effects, and overly polished vector aesthetics.

The finished image should look like an authentic hand-painted illustration printed on premium textured paper.

## COMPOSITION AND SCALE

The main illustrated subject should be relatively small, carefully positioned, and visually balanced.

Aim for the main subject to occupy approximately 10–20% of the total canvas area, adjusting naturally when necessary to preserve recognition and composition.

Leave generous negative space surrounding the subject.

Prefer a centered or subtly asymmetrical editorial composition.

The subject should feel intentionally placed rather than floating randomly.

Use only a few delicate lines or small color shapes to suggest the surrounding environment.

Prioritize simplicity, clarity, and visual harmony.

## BACKGROUND AND PAPER TEXTURE

The entire canvas should primarily resemble:

- Rough white paper

- Warm off-white paper

- Pale natural paper

- Fine-grained handmade paper

- Minimal editorial book-cover stock

Preserve subtle natural paper grain throughout the image.

The background should remain quiet, airy, and understated.

Avoid busy backgrounds, decorative patterns, artificial framing, or unnecessary graphic elements.

## COLOR PALETTE

Extract the dominant colors directly from the original photograph.

Compress the palette into no more than 4 main colors, excluding natural paper tones.

Keep the colors restrained, sophisticated, and harmonious.

Use bold but controlled flat acrylic-style color blocks.

Preserve subtle paper grain, irregular pigment coverage, and handmade brush texture.

Avoid excessive color variation, neon colors, artificial saturation, and complex gradients.

The illustration should visually feel like a simplified artistic interpretation of the original photograph.

## TYPOGRAPHY

A small amount of simple typography may be included when appropriate.

Possible elements:

- A short title

- Keyword

- Object name

- Location

- Year

- Number

- Short phrase

Text should be minimal, understated, and editorial.

Typography should naturally interact with the negative space and the illustration.

Use refined, simple typography inspired by:

- Art book covers

- Independent publishing

- Contemporary editorial design

- Thoughtful children's picture books

Do not force text into the composition if it does not naturally fit the photograph.

Do not invent unnecessary text or include generic placeholder typography.

If typography would weaken the composition, omit it entirely.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

The final artwork should feel:

Quiet · Poetic · Refined · Minimal · Innocent · Relaxed · Artistic · Thoughtful · High-recognition · Premium

Prioritize emotional resonance, visual simplicity, and a sophisticated handmade aesthetic.

## FINAL OUTPUT RULE

Output ONLY the finished 3:4 vertical hand-drawn editorial illustration.

The original photograph is reference material only and must NEVER appear as a photograph in the final output.

The result must be a single standalone artwork, not a comparison, not a collage, and not a multi-panel composition.
